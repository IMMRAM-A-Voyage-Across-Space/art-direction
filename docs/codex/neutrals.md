## How the wild works

**No banner. Den-only. Open to all.** Neutrals belong to no cause. They have no dropship bay and no
faction dwelling — the **only** way to field them is to find and flag their **wild den** on the
isle's surface. Whichever host claims it recruits them. They reward scouting the isle over turtling
at base.

- **Apex wildlife** — One per biome, the isle's native apex predator. Its den appears only on that
  biome's isles. Powerful, expensive, unmistakably local.
- **Universal mercenaries** — Cross-biome outliers: outlaws, rogue machines, sellswords. Rare dens
  that can surface on any isle, regardless of biome.
- **Cause-agnostic** — Neutrals ignore faction buffs and allegiances. They're a flat, flexible way
  for any commander to diversify a host.
