# IMMRAM — Neutral Myths

> Legends of the creatures that belong to no cause. Companion to [world backstories](world-backstories.md)
> and the [faction codex](../factions.md) (the neutral roster). Neutrals are
> recruited only from **wild dens** on the overworld, available to whoever flags them — the things older
> than the war, that every faction tells stories about and none of them own.

---

## Apex Wildlife
*One per isle — the monster the isle itself made.*

### Glas Gaibhleann — the Grazer-Titan · Mag Mell · melee
> "The plain was theirs first. We only ever borrowed it."

Older than the landing, the Grazer-Titans walked Mag Mell's green belts before the clan-host broke a
single acre — migratory mountains of muscle and patience the first settlers learned to plant
*around* rather than fight. They ignore the war entirely; they have outlived every banner that ever
flew here and will outlive this one. Wake one, aim it at a line, and watch the ground remember what
it was before fences: a single charge tramples an entire rank flat.

### Gaoth Sí — the Pollen Wraith · Tír na nÓg · flyer
> "The Druids do not speak of the Wraith. They shudder."

A drifting veil of luminous spores with no body to kill, the Pollen Wraith unmakes *thought* —
soldiers who breathe it forget which way the enemy lies, fire on their own, wander into the tide.
The Grafted have tried for generations to harvest it and come back changed, or not at all. It is the
one thing on a living isle that even the living isle is afraid of: a confusion you cannot armor
against.

### Oilliphéist — the Dune Serpent · Gorias · melee
> "Don't map where it is. Map where it was, and stay clear."

Scrappers swim wide of the Dune Serpent's hunting grounds. It moves through the rust like water,
acid sloughing off scales older than the first drowned hull, and surfaces *under* whatever's
heaviest and most foolish — no wall, no wreck, no cover means anything to a thing that strikes from
below. Na Fomóiri leave it offerings of scrap. The Corrosion calls it a saint.

### Seaca-Phéist — the Hoarfrost Drake · Murias · flyer
> "The first winter had a shape. And wings."

Dún Seaca ("the Frostbound Hold") swears the winter that should have killed the landing was *alive*. The Hoarfrost
Drake is the cold given flight — a breath that freezes a whole line where it stands, a shadow
crossing the white that leaves statues behind. Both causes fight near its roosts and never *for*
them. You do not tame the Drake; you borrow its winter and pray it doesn't notice you're warm.

### Aillén — the Magma Leviathan · Tech Duinn · melee
> "The fire is god. The Leviathan is what answers when it's angry."

It sleeps in the deep lava channels and surfaces in eruption — a slow mountain of living magma that
doesn't so much attack as *arrive*, leaving everything within reach simply on fire. Na Gaibhne ("the Emberwrights")
raise shrines over its resting places. Clann na Luaithe ("the Cinderborn") dive into the lava to be closer to it and are
never seen again, which they count as a success.

### Cú Sí — the Void Stalker · Falias · melee
> "The Order didn't take the vow of silence for nothing. The reason hunts the Veil."

The Void Stalker is the predator the crater-mist seems made to hide: unseen until the instant it
isn't, a single total strike out of the grey, then nothing again. The guides who lead hosts through
the Veil aren't only avoiding the gas — they're avoiding *it*. The Silent Order does not fear the
dark. On Falias, the dark has teeth.

## Wandering Mercenaries
*Found at rare wild dens on any isle — loyal to whoever pays, blind to every cause.*

### Muir-fhoghlaithe — the Drifter Corsairs · ranged
> "We fly no flag. Flags stop paying."

Outlaw gunmen who sell to whoever's winning and rob whoever's losing — and on a good day that's the
same host. Loot is their only loyalty; aim them at a rich enemy and they fight like believers,
because the dead leave their pockets behind (every kill lines the Corsairs' purse, and yours).

### An Sluagh — the Rogue Drone Swarm · flyer
> "It forgot its orders. Then it wrote new ones."

Someone's war-drones, once — whose, no one remembers. The swarm answers only to whoever speaks its
corrupted code, and in the field it turns an enemy's own steel against it, hijacking a mechanical
stack mid-battle. Field them carelessly and they'll do the same to yours.

### Na Díbergaigh — the Ronin Blades · melee
> "Strike clean. Strike first. Take no answer."

Veteran sellswords with one rule left from whatever code they abandoned. The Ronin cut and are gone
before the wound can swing back — they strike without ever provoking retaliation. They don't ask
what the war is about. They ask what it pays, and whether you'll waste their edge.

### Fathach — the Derelict War-Mech · melee
> "It's still following its last order. No one's sure what it was."

Pulled from a crater or a dead hull, the War-Mech predates the factions and maybe the landing
itself — a slab of armor running on a war that ended before anyone alive was born. It takes
punishment like geology and returns it the same way: slowly, hugely, and without apparent opinion on
the matter.

### Lucht na Marbhna — the Void Psalmists · ranged
> "Hire them and you'll win battles you should have lost. You won't enjoy the music."

Robed casters who sing to the vacuum — and sometimes the vacuum sings back. Rare even in legend,
their hymns harden an ally's resolve or unstring an enemy's, drawn from a faith no surviving order
admits to founding.
